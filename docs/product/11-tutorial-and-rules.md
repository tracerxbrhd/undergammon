# 11. Tutorial and Rules

## Accepted decisions

- Tutorials are optional and tracked separately for Long Nardy and Backgammon.
- On first use of a ruleset, the player may choose a short tutorial or start playing immediately.
- Completed or skipped tutorials can be opened again later from the rules/help section.
- The quick tutorial teaches only the mechanics needed to start playing: dice roll, movement direction, checker selection, legal destinations, required dice usage, doubles where relevant, bearing off, and winning.
- Detailed and uncommon cases belong in a separate full Rules Reference.
- In-match help is available through a compact help action and may explain the relevant rule, but must not recommend the best strategic move.
- Tutorial scenarios are deterministic prepared positions, not normal matches against AI.
- Tutorial scenarios reuse the production game engine and legality checks. There is no second simplified copy of the rules.
- Normal legal-move highlighting remains available during real matches and is not treated as strategic assistance.
- Tutorial completion is not a repeatable source of Coins or other farmable rewards.

## Product principle

The tutorial helps a new player become capable of starting a real match without forcing every user through a long onboarding flow. Canonical game rules remain unchanged; UX explains them instead of simplifying them.
